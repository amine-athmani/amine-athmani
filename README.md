### Amine Athmani, PhD

Optimization scientist in Strasbourg, France. I build planning and scheduling engines that run in production: rostering, timetabling, routing and blending. I work on MILP, constraint programming and metaheuristics, and lately on LLM agents that write and repair solver code.

Technical lead at [Dots & Lines](https://dotsandlines.ai), an optimization and AI consultancy. PhD in AI and operations research (UTT, industrial thesis with Adesoft).

**Current work**

- Lead solver engineer on the shift design, rostering and sector assignment service for 100+ air-traffic controllers at Avinor (Java, Timefold, MIP, Kubernetes on Azure).
- [VibeSolve](https://github.com/vibesolve/vibesolve): describe a planning problem in a paragraph, get a tested Timefold solver project. Agents write the model, then check and repair the code until it solves.
- [Timestral in the browser](https://github.com/amine-athmani/timestral-in-browser): time series forecasting with Mistral's Timestral models, run locally with onnxruntime-web. [Live demo](https://amine-athmani.github.io/timestral-in-browser/).

**Open source and research**

- [pyscheduling](https://github.com/scheduling-cc/pyscheduling): Python framework for single machine, parallel machine, flow shop and job shop scheduling, with exact, CP and metaheuristic solvers (GECCO 2023 Companion).
- [ITC2021](https://github.com/amine-athmani/ITC2021): our entry to the International Timetabling Competition 2021 (sports scheduling).
- Papers at GECCO 2022, OLA 2023, META 2021 and CPAIOR 2022 on ML-guided scheduling and metaheuristic tuning. [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mZMSkxUAAAAJ).
